# Project Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
People sizing up Ibrahim Shittu — hiring managers, founders, senior engineers,
potential collaborators — usually arriving from a link (LinkedIn, X, email
signature) to answer one question fast: *is this person's work serious?* They
skim, then read. The site must work as a 5-second scan and a calm long read.

### Brand Personality
- **Three words:** precise, calm, premium.
- **Voice:** first-person, plain, understated. No hype, no marketing slogans.
  Lets the work and the craft of the page speak.
- **Emotional goal:** "this person has taste and ships quality." Trust through
  restraint and obvious attention to detail — not decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction — "Refined dark" (Linear / Vercel register)
Premium engineered dark. The quality comes from **typography, spacing, and
restraint**, not ornament. Dark is the default; a light theme exists via toggle.

- **Type:** **Geist Sans** for everything (display, body, UI) + **Geist Mono**
  for micro-labels, dates, and code. No serif. The signature move is a large,
  tightly-tracked display heading against small, quiet mono labels.
- **Palette (monochrome, with depth — no accent color):**
  - Dark (default): bg `#0a0a0b`, raised `#161618`, text `#ededec`,
    secondary `#8c8b86`, faint `#5c5b56`, hairline `#1c1c1e`, hairline-strong `#2a2a2d`.
  - Light: bg `#faf9f7`, text `#1a1917`, secondary `#6b6660`, faint `#9b958c`,
    hairline `#e9e6e0`.
  - Never pure black or pure white. Links/affordances are shown by brightening
    to full foreground + a hover arrow, NOT by a color.
- **Layout:** single centered reading column (`max-w` 48rem), generous top
  space, hairline dividers between rows, mono section labels (WORK, SELECTED
  WORK, WRITING). Sticky translucent nav with backdrop blur.
- **Motion:** subtle only — hover arrow slides in on rows, links brighten.
  Calm, never bouncy.

### Type scale (premium intent)
- Display H1: clamp ~2.5rem → 3.75rem, weight 600, tracking -0.04em, leading 1.02.
- Section label: Geist Mono, 11px, uppercase, tracking 0.2em, faint.
- Row title: 1.0625rem, weight 500, tracking -0.01em, foreground.
- Period / role: Geist Mono, 12–13px, tabular-nums, faint.
- Body / summary: 15–18px, line-height 1.6–1.7, secondary color.

### Design Principles
1. **Precision over decoration.** Every line, gap, and weight is deliberate.
   Remove before adding.
2. **One typeface, used with conviction.** Big size contrast (display vs mono
   label) is the whole personality. No second display font.
3. **Monochrome with depth.** Layered near-blacks and three text tints carry the
   hierarchy; no accent color needed.
4. **Mono is the accent.** Tracked uppercase mono labels and tabular dates give
   the engineered, premium-developer feel — used sparingly.
5. **Calm motion.** A single restrained hover interaction per row; nothing flashy.
6. **The essay is the product.** Long-form reading (blog) gets the most care:
   comfortable measure, real rhythm, quiet furniture.
